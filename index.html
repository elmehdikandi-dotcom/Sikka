    Find a station

    How positions are estimated. Each train is placed on the track by interpolating between its departure and arrival times, with acceleration out of stations and braking into them. Without a live feed, timetables are indicative, modeled on typical ONCF service patterns. With a feed, each train runs on its published times shifted by its reported delay. Neither gives GPS positions. Check real times on oncf-voyages.ma.
